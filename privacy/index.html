Privacy Terms

Privacy policy · effective 23 October 2026

Motivify collects nothing.

This policy covers the Motivify app for Android and this website. "We" means the developer of Motivify.

In short

Motivify does not collect or share any data. There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no trackers. Everything you do in the app stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

Motivify keeps these on your device only. They are never sent to us or to anyone else:

  • the passages you save;
  • your habits and their history;
  • the practices you complete, your streak, XP, ranks and milestones;
  • your progress in programs;
  • your reminders and settings, such as theme, weekly goal and the themes you read.

What leaves your phone

Nothing is sent by the app. Motivify has no internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere.

When you tap Share, the app makes a card of the passage and hands it to Android's share sheet. It goes only where you choose to send it, and the shared text includes a link to Motivify on Google Play. When you tap Copy, the passage goes to your clipboard.

Backups

If Android backup is on, Android may include Motivify's data in your device backup in your own Google account, or copy it when you move to a new phone. This is handled by Android under Google's terms. We cannot access these backups.

Notifications and widgets

Reminders are scheduled on your phone, and widgets are drawn on your phone. Neither uses a server.

Ratings

Motivify may ask you to rate it. That prompt is shown by the Google Play Store app, not by Motivify, and nothing is sent to us. A review you post is public on Google Play and is covered by the Google privacy policy.

Children

Motivify collects no data from anyone, of any age, so it has no age check.

This website

This website uses no cookies and no scripts, loads nothing from other sites and has no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may keep server logs, such as IP addresses, to run the service. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Delete your data

Open Settings → Apps → Motivify → Storage and tap Clear storage, or uninstall Motivify. Either removes everything the app kept on your phone. You can manage or delete your device backups in your Google account's backup settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
